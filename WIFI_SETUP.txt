================================================================================
   ESP32 HYDROPONICS NODE - WI-FI & MQTT SETUP GUIDE
================================================================================

1. POWER ON THE ESP32
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Connect the ESP32 to a 5V power supply or USB port.
- On first boot (or if it cannot find saved Wi-Fi within 20 seconds), the 
  built-in status LED (GPIO 2) stays SOLID ON.
- This indicates the pairing access point is active.


2. CONNECT TO THE SETUP HOTSPOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- On your phone or PC, open your Wi-Fi settings.
- Connect to the broadcasted network:
    SSID:     Hydro-Setup
    Password: hydro1234


3. OPEN THE CONFIGURATION PORTAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Most devices will display a "Sign in to network" pop-up automatically.
- If it doesn't open automatically:
    1. Open any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox).
    2. Go to: http://192.168.4.1
    3. Click/Tap "Configure WiFi".


4. CONFIGURE WI-FI & MQTT BROKER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Select your home/local 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network from the scanned list.
- Enter your Wi-Fi Password.
- Configure your MQTT Broker parameters:
    * MQTT broker host / IP : LAN IP of your broker (e.g., 192.168.1.105)
                              Do NOT use "localhost" or "127.0.0.1".
    * MQTT port             : 1883
    * Username / Password   : (Optional) Enter credentials if enabled, 
                              otherwise leave blank.


5. SAVE & VERIFY CONNECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tap "Save".
- The ESP32 will write the parameters to flash (LittleFS & NVS) and reboot.
- Once connected:
    * The status LED will turn OFF.
    * The LED will briefly flash every 10 seconds whenever telemetry publishes.
- In the Arduino Serial Monitor (115200 baud), verify:
    [WiFi] Connected to '<Your_SSID>', IP 192.168.x.x
    [MQTT] Connected and subscribed


================================================================================
   RE-ENTERING SETUP / RE-PAIRING LATER
================================================================================
- If you change your Wi-Fi router or MQTT IP:
    1. Keep the ESP32 powered on.
    2. Press and hold the onboard "BOOT" button (GPIO 0) for 5 seconds.
    3. All dosing pumps will shut off for safety, and the "Hydro-Setup" 
       hotspot will reopen for 3 minutes.
================================================================================
